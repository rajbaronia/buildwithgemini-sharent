SHARENT Standard Master Rental Agreement (v1.0) BINDING CONTRACT

1. Item Care, Safety & Operating Compliance

The Renter agrees to operate the rented item strictly in compliance with the manufacturer’s operating manual, provided guides, and safety instructions. The Renter agrees to wear appropriate protective gear and refrain from sub-leasing, loaning, or utilizing the equipment for illegal or commercial purposes.

2. Security Deposit Escrow & Return Inspection

The refundable security deposit is held safely in SHARENT platform escrow. Upon returning the item to the Owner at the agreed return time in clean, functioning condition (ordinary wear-and-tear excepted), the deposit is released 100% back to the Renter within 24 hours of owner inspection.

3. Timely Return & Late Penalty Policy

The item must be returned by the agreed return date. Unapproved late returns are subject to a late fee equal to 1.5× the daily base rate for each 24-hour period beyond the scheduled reservation window.

4. Accidental Damage, Loss & Insurance Protection

If the item is covered under SHARENT Equipment Protection, repair costs resulting from accidental damage during regular usage are subsidized per platform policy. The Renter remains liable for intentional misuse, neglect, or loss up to the verified replacement value.

5. Dispute Mediation & Digital Acceptance

Both Owner and Renter agree to resolve any damage claims or discrepancies through the SHARENT Concierge Dispute Mediation system. Digital acknowledgment creates a legally binding peer-to-peer agreement between both parties.
